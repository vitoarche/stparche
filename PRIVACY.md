# Privacy Policy for STP&ARCHE Bot

**Effective Date:** 01/02/2026

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect the following information to provide our services:
- **User ID & Username**: To identify users for leveling and moderation commands.
- **Guild ID & Name**: To configure settings specific to your server.
- **Message Content**: Only processed temporarily to execute commands (e.g., playing music, filtering spam). We do not store message history.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To execute requested commands (e.g., playing music log, banning users).
- To maintain the "Leveling System" (storing XP and Rank data).

## 3. Data Storage
- Data is stored in a secure local database (`SQLite`).
- You may request the deletion of your data by contacting the developer.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
- We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as required by Discord's Terms of Service or law.
- Music playback relies on third-party services like YouTube and Spotify; their privacy policies apply when fetching content.

## 5. Contact
For questions or data deletion requests, please contact the bot owner via the STP Discord Server.
